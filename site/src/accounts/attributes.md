# Account titles and their attributes

`AccountTitles` is the element type for account titles. Each constructor, such as `Cash`, is an element. The wildcard element `AccountTitle` has no account attributes. Both `accountSpec AccountTitle` and `accountSemantics AccountTitle` return `Nothing`.

## Account specification

Use `accountSpec` to get the `AccountSpec` record for a concrete account title.

- `asDivision` gives the division. The values are `Assets` for assets, `Liability` for liabilities, `Equity` for equity, `Revenue` for revenue, and `Cost` for expenses.
- `asClosing` gives the automatic closing rule. `CloseByDivision` uses the division to determine eligibility and transfer direction. `NoClose` skips automatic closing for the account.
- `asIsContra` identifies contra accounts, which offset balances in their division.
- `asFixedCurrent` classifies the account as `Fixed`, `Current`, or `Other`. For example, revenue and expense accounts use `Other`.
- `asNameEn` gives the English name. `asNameJa` gives the Japanese name, which may include notes. `asLabelJa` gives the Japanese display name without notes.
- `asDescription` gives a description of the account.
- `asAliases` lists the aliases in the registry. `accountAliases` combines these with aliases from the Japan Chamber of Commerce and Industry's account names.

## Account semantics

Use `accountSemantics` to get the `AccountSemantics` record for bookkeeping and reporting.

- `asemRoles` lists the roles of the account. An account can have multiple roles. `OrdinaryAccount` marks an ordinary account, `ContraAccount` offsets another balance, and `ReciprocalAccount` matches balances between entities. `SuspenseOrClearingAccount` holds amounts temporarily. `ClosingDevice` supports closing entries. `AttributionAccount` assigns amounts to a group. `PeriodResult` represents a period result and `ReportingSubtotal` represents a subtotal.
- `asemPostingCapability` describes where the account may be used. `OrdinaryPosting` allows ordinary entries. `ClosingOnly`, `ConsolidationOnly`, and `EngineGeneratedOnly` limit use to closing, consolidation, and entries generated by the library, respectively. `NotPostable` marks an account unavailable for recording entries.
- `asemDivisionSemantics` explains what the division represents. `StatementDivision` gives a financial statement classification. `BookkeepingControlClass` gives a class used to control a bookkeeping procedure. `DirectionEncoding` uses the division to encode direction. `NoStatementDivision` indicates that no statement division applies.
- `asemHomeSideSemantics` describes the home side, the debit or credit side on which you record an increase in the account. `FixedHomeSide` specifies `Debit` or `Credit`. `ContextDependentHomeSide` depends on the context. `NoFixedHomeSide` gives no single home side. `NoPostingSide` means that no posting side applies.
- `asemReportingEligibility` describes eligibility for financial statement presentation. `StatementEligible` marks an eligible account. `ContextualPresentation` depends on the reporting context. `DerivedPresentation` represents a derived result or subtotal. `NotPresented` excludes the account from presentation.

## Where the attributes are used

- [Closing entries](../bookkeeping/closing.md) use the closing rule and division to select accounts to close. The division and contra status determine the transfer direction.
- [Financial statements](../bookkeeping/statements.md) use the statement division and the fixed/current classification to group lines. The `ContraAccount` role determines whether a balance is presented as a deduction.
- [Reading journals from CSV](../io/input.md) uses `accountAliases` to resolve alternate names.
- [Trial balance and checks](../bookkeeping/trial-balance.md) use the home side to flag ordinary accounts with a balance on the opposite side. They use roles to check balances in accounts such as suspense and clearing accounts.

## English names in the built-in chart

Each account in the [built-in chart](builtin.md) has a unique English name. For example, `RetainedEarnings` is “Retained earnings brought forward” and `EarnedSurplus` is “Retained earnings”. The English name of `Building` is “Buildings”.
